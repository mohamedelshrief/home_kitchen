# Engineering Review — Current Option 2

Review against the owner's reconfirmed plan. This records findings; it does not approve fabrication or alter the layout.

## Confirmed plan reading

Room width 2210 mm; length 2250 + 700 = 2950 mm. Window-side usable wall 1300 + 710 = 2010 mm, giving a 200 mm projection over the last 700 mm. West side: 700 mm return, 750 mm entrance opening, 1500 mm return. The kitchen ceiling, entrance head and window heights are still unmeasured. The 2470 mm ceiling came from a bedroom measurement.

## Findings and remedies

| Finding | Evidence / consequence | Proposed response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entry restriction | Nominal refrigerator depth 840 mm exceeds the 700 mm return by 140 mm; the straight approach beside it reduces locally from 750 to about 610 mm before protruding handles. User accepted this projection for the concept, but that does not eliminate its effect. | Tape out the appliance and approach on site. To improve access, revisit its position or consider a shallower appliance; do not assume the doorway can be widened structurally. |
| Narrow work aisle | 2210 - 650 - 650 = 910 mm between opposing counters. Against the column: 2010 - 650 - 450 = 910 mm. | Prefer one-person use; assess appliance projections. Two 600 mm runs would only yield 1010 mm and may not fit the current appliances/services. Reaching roughly 1070 mm needs about 160 mm reduction across opposing depths or removal of one run. |
| Dishwasher blocks circulation when open | Modelled open door spans x=635–1373 mm. Opposite counter starts at x=1560 mm, leaving about 187 mm at counter-front projection. No collision with the closed range is not evidence of usable passing space. | Preserve the sink-side service zone but plan loading as a temporary blocked-aisle operation. Check the extended racks and the real appliance drawing. Do not assume the oven and dishwasher can be open together; the oven is not animated. |
| Corner standing space | Only about 240 mm separates the dishwasher end at z=2060 mm from the perpendicular south counter front at z=2300 mm. | Rework the wet-zone modules before joinery. Moving the dishwasher toward the entrance to gain corner clearance trades away entry access. |

Hiding whole fronts in Corner Interiors is not a working door mechanism. | Specify a real door/access layout and test with stored items. A smaller accessible cabinet can be more useful than a large inaccessible corner. Select commercial corner hardware only after checking its required opening and cabinet envelope. |
| Refrigerator animation is incorrect | In the current north-facing placement, the viewer rotates the right-hinged door by -90 degrees. Check refrigerator drawers and the final hinge/handle installation against manufacturer drawings. This audit records the defect without silently changing the model. |
| Short preparation and landing zones | Main east straight preparation section is about 600 mm; the cooker has only about 150 mm toward the south return. This may require redistributing cabinets or, if the owner chooses, changing the 900 mm range size. |
| AC installation not established | 800 × 270 × 210 mm envelope, bottom 2050 mm, top 2320 mm, with assumed ceiling 2470 mm and entrance head 2000 mm. Check selected-unit top/side/service clearances, filter removal, condensate routing and airflow relative to the gas cooker. |
| Raised microwave is a study | The 544 × 308 × 458 mm appliance is shown on a shelf, not validated as an enclosed or suspended installation. | Confirm ventilation, shelf load/anchorage and safe reach using the actual LG instructions. |

## Plumbing assessment

The dishwasher is now on the west return beside the sink corner. Supply and waste can be coordinated in that same zone rather than routed around the refrigerator. The open floor allows route selection, but final waste levels, fall, trap, access and dishwasher connection/loop requirements still need the plumber and appliance instructions. Multiple changes in direction are not intrinsically the deciding fault; avoid unnecessarily long routes and inaccessible joints.

## Recommendation

Keep this as a comparison concept. Before fabrication, resolve the refrigerator approach, correct its door simulation, and select a genuinely accessible corner/door arrangement. Do not promise all full-depth runs, a perfectly centred sink, maximum corner volume and generous appliance circulation simultaneously in this footprint. If the owner retains all current appliances and the accepted entry obstruction, acknowledge the resulting compact one-person layout and prioritise access over gross cabinet volume.

## Planning reference

NKBA's published Kitchen Planning Guidelines recommend about 1067 mm work aisle for one cook, 914 × 610 mm continuous preparation surface beside a sink, and 533 mm standing space between a dishwasher and perpendicular cabinets/counters. These are design benchmarks, not a statement of applicable Egyptian building regulations: https://media.nkba.org/uploads/2022/05/Kitchen-Planning-Guidelines.pdf
